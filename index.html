The difference

Most sleep advice comes from coaches.
Ours comes from a pharmacist.

Sleep coaches can give you routines. But if your sleep is disrupted by medications, hormones, anxiety, or a new baby — you need someone who understands the clinical picture, not just the bedtime habits.

The Wellness Script bridges the gap between clinical pharmacy and everyday wellbeing. Real science. Practical tools. A pharmacist who actually listens.

Medication affecting your sleep?

Beta-blockers, steroids, antidepressants, diuretics — many common medications disrupt sleep. A pharmacist can review yours and suggest practical solutions.

Postnatal exhaustion & sleep disruption

New mothers face a unique combination of sleep deprivation, hormonal changes and often medication. We understand this intersection better than a generic sleep app.

Hormonal sleep changes

Perimenopause, menstrual cycles and thyroid conditions all affect sleep quality. We look at the whole picture, not just the surface symptoms.

Supplements — what actually works?

Magnesium glycinate, melatonin, ashwagandha — we cut through the noise and tell you what the evidence says, and whether it's safe with your medications.
